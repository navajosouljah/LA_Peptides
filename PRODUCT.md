# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Internal stakeholders and clinical/wholesale buyers evaluating LA Peptides against
other US-synthesized (domestic SPPS) peptide vendors. They are reading a dense
price-comparison table to answer one question fast: where does LA Peptides win on
price and on $/mg, and where is it the only option. Context is a sales/strategy
review, often shared on a screen or in a deck. Tied to the PepX strategic brief,
where LA Peptides is the upstream peptide supplier and "credibility tail."

## Product Purpose

A single-screen competitive market analysis. It catalogs every LA Peptides SKU and
mg variant across seven categories, shows the per-mg breakdown, places three
US-synthesized competitors side by side, and flags LA's price position vs the
market. Success = a buyer or partner can scan one category and immediately see the
value story without reading prose.

## Brand Personality

Clinical, precise, trustworthy. The voice of a lab certificate, not a supplement
ad. Scientific authority that is still approachable. Three words: precise,
credible, clean. The interface should feel like documentation you would hand to an
attorney, matching PepX's "put the lab on the cover" positioning.

## Anti-references

- Generic dark "AI SaaS dashboard" (near-black bg, neon accents, glassmorphism).
  This is what the first version looked like and what we are explicitly leaving.
- Supplement/nootropic DTC hype aesthetics (loud gradients, urgency, lifestyle).
- Crypto/fintech terminal dark mode.

## Design Principles

- **Read like a lab report.** Legibility and tabular precision over decoration.
  Numbers align, units are consistent, the eye finds the LA column instantly.
- **The subject is LA Peptides.** Visual weight steers to LA's price and $/mg;
  competitors are present but quiet reference points.
- **Quiet confidence.** The value story is in the data, not in shouting. Restrained
  color, one brand accent doing the work.
- **On-brand, not on-trend.** Match lapeptides.net's real light/clinical identity
  rather than a fashionable dashboard look.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Body/data text ≥4.5:1; savings badges legible by shape+text, not color
alone (↓/↑ glyphs and % accompany the color). Full `prefers-reduced-motion`
fallback. Table scrolls horizontally on small screens rather than reflowing data.
